Privacy Policy for ScanEasy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

ScanEasy ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the ScanEasy mobile application collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

ScanEasy is designed to scan and manage documents. We may collect:

- Camera access to scan documents.
- Storage access to save and open scanned files.
- Basic device information (such as device model and operating system) to improve app performance.

We do not collect or store the contents of your scanned documents on our servers unless you explicitly choose to upload or back them up.

We use collected information to:

- Provide document scanning features.
- Improve app performance and stability.

Data Sharing

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You can:

- Revoke camera or storage permissions at any time through your device settings.
- Delete scanned files stored on your device whenever you choose.

Children's Privacy

ScanEasy is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Privacy Policy for ScanEasy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

ScanEasy ("the App") is a document scanning application that enables users to scan paper documents, enhance scans, create PDF files, save PDFs locally on their device, and share those files using the sharing features available on their device.

1. Information We Collect

ScanEasy does not collect, store, transmit, sell, rent, or otherwise process personal information on our servers. The app does not require account registration and does not request your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.

2. Camera Permission

The Camera permission is used only to capture images of documents for scanning. Images remain on your device unless you intentionally share them.

3. Storage Access

Storage access is used only to save, open, import, or manage PDF files and scanned images on your device. Your files remain under your control.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your information or documents with third parties. When you choose to share a PDF, the file is shared only through the app or service you select.

5. Third-Party Services

ScanEasy does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs to collect personal information. If a future version introduces such services, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

6. Data Security

Because your documents are stored locally on your device, protecting access to your device helps protect your files. We recommend using your device's built-in security features.

7. Children's Privacy

ScanEasy is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
